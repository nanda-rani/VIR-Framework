\section{Open Science}
\label{app:open-science}

We provide an anonymous artifact at
\url{ANONYMOUS_ARTIFACT_URL}. The artifact contains the complete Victim
Interpretation and Response (VIR) codebook and adjudication protocol, a
machine-readable label schema, aggregate-analysis and evaluation code, a
machine-readable record of the aggregate results reported in the paper, and a
wholly synthetic dataset for exercising the public pipeline. The synthetic
records were invented for the artifact and are neither NCRP records nor
paraphrases of individual complaints. The repository includes a quick-start
guide, an artifact-to-claim manifest, small-cell suppression, and automated
privacy and anonymization checks. It also provides a complete implementation
of the method described in the paper: a partially frozen shared HingRoBERTa
encoder with task-specific projection and prediction heads, weighted
multi-task losses, focal loss for misconception, deterministic stratified
partitioning, validation-loss early stopping, validation-set threshold tuning,
held-out testing, and label propagation. The configuration records all model,
optimization, loss, split, and decoding settings used by the artifact.

We cannot distribute the underlying complaint corpus. The data consist of
118,848 anonymized victim reports submitted to India's National Cybercrime
Reporting Portal and made available through the CyberGuard AI Hackathon 2024,
organized by IndiaAI and the Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre. We are not
the data owners and do not have authorization to redistribute these records.
Moreover, free-text narratives concerning victim experiences can retain
indirect identifiers and distinctive event details after direct identifiers
have been removed. We therefore exclude the raw complaints; the human-labelled
and model-labelled complaint-level datasets; record identifiers and split
memberships; embeddings, token caches, and raw model outputs; and other
intermediate representations from which complaint information might be
recovered. We also withhold the fine-tuned model weights because memorization
of sensitive narratives has not been ruled out. Researchers seeking the NCRP
data must obtain authorization independently from the relevant data controller;
we cannot grant or guarantee such access.

Upon acceptance, all releasable materials will be deposited in a versioned
archive providing persistent public access, and this appendix will be updated
with its stable, non-anonymous URL and version. The data restrictions apply
only to complaint-level materials and do not prevent permanent release of the
codebook, schema, code, documentation, synthetic examples, and aggregate
results.
